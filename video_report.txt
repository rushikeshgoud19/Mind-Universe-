### RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **PING-PONG LOOPING NEEDED:** Because all ambient scenes are rated POOR for standard hard-looping (due to continuous one-directional rotation and particle physics), the developer MUST implement a "Ping-Pong" loop playback strategy during the idle states (e.g., play from 0.0s to 8.0s, then play in reverse from 8.0s back to 0.0s). This will create a seamless, breathing "zero-gravity" effect without any visual cuts.
2. **SPEED ADJUSTMENT:** The transition pans (the 7-8 second windows moving into the portals) are beautifully paced, but tie the scroll-jacking strictly to `currentTime` mapping so the user controls the velocity of the push-in.
